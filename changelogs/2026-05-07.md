## Search and title pages

### Find anything
- Search movies, shows, collections, and people — no account needed.
- Every title, show, collection, and person has its own page with trailers, details, and cast.
- Pick your country once and every page shows what's available where you live. Sign in and Watchly remembers it.

### Where to watch
- Each title shows where it's streaming, renting, or for sale in your country.
- When the usual listings don't cover your country, Watchly checks JustWatch so you still get an answer, and tells you where the info came from.
- If a title isn't listed for your country at all, we say so instead of showing services you can't use.
- Service names are cleaned up, so you see "Prime Video" and "Disney+", not long store names.
- On search results, hover a poster to see where it's streaming.

## For You

### Tonight's Briefing
- A personal feed built from your favorites and watchlists, led by one big pick for tonight.
- Not feeling the pick? **Spin Again** for another. Already seen it? **Mark as Watched** and it won't come back.
- Rows include **From Your Favorites**, **Because you added…**, **Up Next From Your Watchlists**, **Popular Right Now**, **Trending This Week**, and **Quick Picks**.
- Filter rows on the spot (Movies, TV Shows, Under 2h, Highly Rated), or open **View All** to see the whole row.
- Tell us which streaming services you have, and picks lean toward what you can actually watch.

## Watchlists

### Your lists, your way
- Make as many watchlists as you like, public or private. Your first one, **My Watchlist**, is ready from day one.
- Add a title to one or several lists in a single step, or add a whole movie collection at once.
- Drag titles to put them in your own order.
- Public lists can be shared with anyone, even people without an account.

### Track where you are
- Give every title a status: Just Added, Up Next, Watching, Waiting for Episode, Waiting for Season, Paused, Finished, Rewatch, or Dropped.
- Watchly notes when you started and finished each title.
- Filter a list by status, or switch to **Board** view and drag titles between status columns.
- Add a short note to any title on your list.

## Favorites

- Favorite movies, shows, collections, and people with one tap.
- Browse your favorites by type and sort them by date added or A→Z.

## Profiles

### Make it yours
- Your profile shows your watchlists, favorites, and recent activity, with a public version for everyone else.
- Upload a photo and pick an accent color. Your color follows you across your profile and your lists.
- A banner made from your favorite posters sits at the top. You can turn it off in settings.

### Your taste, at a glance
- Your profile sums up the genres and eras you love most, and the services you watch on.
- Your country appears on your profile.

## Friends

- Follow people to keep up with what they're watching. Private and followers-only profiles ask for approval first.
- Approve or decline follow requests from your inbox.
- See who follows you and who you follow, find people you might know, and spot friends you share.
- Block anyone you don't want to hear from.

## Activity and notifications

- Your activity feed collects what you've favorited and added, grouped into Today, Yesterday, This Week, and Older.
- Tap any activity to jump straight to that title or list.
- The bell lights up as soon as something new happens, like a follow request or a friend's activity.
- Choose which notifications you get in settings.

## Getting started

- Sign up, then pick a few titles you love so For You has something to work with from the first visit.
- Verify your email with one tap from your inbox.
- Forgot your password? Reset it by email.
- Deleted your account by mistake? You can restore it within 30 days.

## Settings

- Choose dark, light, or your system's theme. Your choice follows you to other devices.
- Control who sees your profile and watchlists, and hide your favorites if you like.
- Change your email or password, manage your services and notifications, or delete your account.
