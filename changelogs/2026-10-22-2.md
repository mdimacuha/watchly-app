## For You

- The trailer is now YouTube's full player: hover it to see the video's title and channel, or open it on YouTube.
- A new pause button sits at the start of the caption bar, next to sound. Tapping the rest of the card and pressing Space still pause too.
- Pausing from inside YouTube's player keeps the reel in step, and tapping the still or the play button picks up where you left off.
- Taps on the trailer now go to YouTube's player. Tap anywhere else on the card to pause, or double tap to like.
- On narrow phones and short windows, the trailer keeps a size you can actually watch.
