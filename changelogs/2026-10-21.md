## For You

### A reel of trailers
- **For You** is now a vertical reel: one title at a time, its trailer playing in a card, and a flick up or down to move on.
- Trailers skip the studio logos, play with captions, and start at half volume.
- Tap the trailer card to pause or play. A play icon shows in the middle only while it's paused.
- Set the volume with the slider by the sound button, not just on or off. Watchly remembers your level on this device.
- Save to **Watchlist** or **Like** a title without leaving the reel. Double tap anywhere on the trailer card to like it.
- On a computer, use the up and down buttons beside the reel, the arrow keys, or J and K to move. Press L to like, Space to pause and M for sound.
- The space around the reel is dimmed so the trailer stands out, and the card stays dark even in light mode.

### More about it
- Tap **More about it** to see where to stream, rent or buy it in your region, change region, see who directed or created it, and jump to its page.

### Picked with variety
- Picks rotate between the different titles you love, so one favorite can't fill the whole reel.
- Only titles with a trailer or clip show up.

## Your profile

### Likes
- A new **Likes** tab sits next to Profile, Library and Reviews, with every title you liked in Watchly Swipe and For You in one place, newest first. Titles you favorited in Watchly Swipe count as likes too.
- Filter by **All**, **Watchly Swipe** or **For You**. A small icon on each poster shows where you liked it.
- On a computer, hover a poster for its title, or click it to see its details beside the wall. On a phone, titles sit right under each poster.
- More likes load as you scroll, inside their own box, so your profile doesn't get any longer.
- Changed your mind? **Unlike** a title from the details or the heart on its poster, with **Undo** if you tap it by mistake.

### Privacy
- Your likes are private by default. Turn on **Show my likes** in Settings › Privacy to show the Likes tab to people who can open your profile.

## Favorites

- The **Wide** view shows every shelf as a card. Its top slowly cycles through the posters on that shelf, with each title's name.
- Click a card to open the shelf right there. It grows into a bigger panel with everything on it, and the other cards move aside.
- Rename a shelf by clicking its name, right on the card or in the open panel. A new shelf you make here opens among the cards, without leaving the view.
- Shelves on your profile come first, nine cards to a page, with arrows to see the rest.
- Your profile's Library shows your favorites and shelves as the same cards.

## Tonight's Programme

- The Feature, Supports and Wildcard picks you know from For You now sit under **Tonight's Programme** in the sidebar.
- Old links to **Go deeper** and the full ranked lists still take you to the right place.
- Opens right away after you swipe or like, instead of making you wait while your picks are rebuilt. Titles you just decided on are already gone, and fresh picks follow on your next visit.

## Watchly Swipe

- New **How it works** guide: what Pass, Like and Favorite each do, how the reel and undo work, and every control for touch and keyboard. It opens on your first visit and stays one tap away in the header.
- Undo now always takes back your latest swipe. Before, undoing right after a fast swipe could bring back the card before it.

## Everywhere

- The section list in **Settings** now follows you all the way down, so Appearance and Your data light up when you reach them.
- The sidebar marks For You as **Beta** and Tonight's Programme as **New**.
- On title pages, **Add to Watchlist**, **Add to Favorites** and **Seen it** now sit together in one group, since they all save the title to your lists.
- **People you might know** only suggests people who have verified their email.
- A watchlist's **Board** view shows four columns at a time on a computer and scrolls a whole column at a time, so a status never ends up half off screen.
- **Favorites**, **Watchlists** and each watchlist's own page no longer stretch edge to edge on wide screens. They now line up with the rest of the app.
- Artist pages show each title once, with all of the person's roles on it (like "Director, Writer, Producer"), instead of one copy of the poster per role.
- The **Add to Watchlist** picker no longer flickers and reloads while it's open, on title pages, Quick View, Watchly Swipe and For You.
