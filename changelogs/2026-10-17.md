## For You

### Tonight's Programme
- For You is now a short, hand-picked programme for tonight: one feature, two supporting picks, and a wildcard to shake things up.
- Tell us how much time you have and what mood you're in, and the programme re-deals to fit.
- Every pick is drawn from your favorites, watchlists, and swipes.

### Go deeper
- Want more? **Go deeper** opens a longer list grouped by **Because you liked…**. Tap the title you liked to Quick View it.
- The top pick in each group gets the spotlight, and **Something different** sits at the end for when you want to step outside your usual taste.

## Search

### Results that understand you
- Search no longer needs the exact title. Partial names and missing accents still find what you mean.
- Search for an actor or director and you'll see them first, with the films and shows they're known for right underneath.
- Searching a theme like "found footage" or "time travel" now brings back titles about that theme, not just titles with those words in the name.
- The match count is now accurate — if it says 3 matching, you'll see 3.

### Faster to use
- Suggestions appear as you type, so you can jump straight to a title or person.
- A clear button empties the search box in one tap.
- Tap a suggested theme to search it instantly.

### Better filters
- The year filter now goes all the way back, not just to 1977.
- The streaming service filter lists the popular services first and now includes every other service available in your region.

### Never a dead end
- When you reach the end of your results, we suggest titles you're likely to enjoy based on what you searched.

## Quick View

### Peek before you commit
- Tapping a title almost anywhere on Watchly now opens a Quick View instead of leaving the page. Use **Go to Page** when you want the full details.
- Quick View shows where to watch first, so you know right away if it's on a service you have.
- Movies show the collection they belong to. Shows list their seasons with episode counts and total run time.
- Tap an actor to see their top roles without losing your place.
- Quick View now fits your phone screen instead of running off the right edge.

## Title pages

### Seasons and crew
- Seasons are now an easy-to-scan list with episode counts, instead of a wall of large posters.
- Each show's page shows its full scale at a glance — for example, "14 seasons · 308 episodes · ~154h total".
- The director, writers, and producers are now listed alongside the cast.
- The page background stays put as you scroll long pages.

## Watchlists

### List view
- Watchlists now have a list view alongside Grid and Board, built for scanning long lists.
- Changing a title's status re-sorts the list right away: Watching first, then Up Next, then the rest.

### Ranked lists
- Curators can now rank their lists. Choose **Auto** to let Watchly sort by status, or **Ranked** to set your own order.
- To rank a title, tap its rank number and type a new position. Enter 0 to unrank it.
- Newly added titles land at the top with a **Recently Added** badge until you rank them.

### On your phone
- A list's buttons now wrap neatly on small screens, so the page no longer slides sideways.

## Watchly Swipe

### Smoother swiping
- Cards no longer get stuck after a fast swipe.
- Dragging with a mouse moves the card instead of dragging the poster image.
- The deck now goes deeper, so you run out of picks less often.

### The Reel
- Every card you swipe drops into a film strip under the deck, so you can see your recent picks at a glance.
- Tap any frame in the Reel to open its Quick View.
- Changed your mind? Edit past swipes from your swipe history.

## Profiles

### Peek at watchlists
- Tap a watchlist on anyone's profile to preview it without leaving the page: who made it, how far through it they are, and what's at the top of the list.
- Tap any title in the preview to open its Quick View, or use **Go to Page** for the full list.

### Followers and following
- Followers and following lists have a fresh look: each person's card wears their own accent color and shows their bio.
- You can now see at a glance who is **Mutual** and who **Follows you**.
- Lists with six or more people can be searched by name or @handle.

### Privacy
- Profiles now fully respect your privacy settings. Hidden favorites, watched titles, and private watchlists stay hidden from visitors, including in profile stats.
- Followers-only profiles now open for your approved followers.

## What's new

- Every update to Watchly is now listed on the [What's new](/changelogs) page, written in plain language. Find it at the bottom of the sidebar.
- New features are announced in the sidebar too. Close the card and it stays closed until the next release.
