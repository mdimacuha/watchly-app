## Profiles

### Your member card
- Your profile now opens with a member card: when you joined, how much you've logged, and a one-line summary of your taste, like "Mostly 2020s horror · Films 70% · Watches on Netflix".
- Stamps show your top genre, era, and streaming service at a glance.
- Choose what the card highlights in **Edit**: what you're watching now, your #1 favorite, or any favorite with a label like "Can't stop rewatching".
- Change your accent color right from the card and watch the page recolor instantly.
- The card tilts gently as you move over it, edged in your accent color.

### Your picks and stats
- **Feature presentation** puts your top favorites front and center, ranked by your own ratings.
- **Programmes** shows your watchlists. Tap one to preview it without leaving the page.
- **By the numbers** breaks down your films vs series, favorite era, what's coming up next, your taste DNA, and your review style.
- Your Watchly Swipe stats appear there too, visible only to you.

### Share it
- Tap the export icon next to **Share** to turn your card and stats into an image sized for an Instagram story. Preview it first, then download it, or on your phone send it straight to Instagram.
- **Preview as visitor** shows your profile exactly as other people see it.

### Visiting someone's profile
- When you're signed in, a profile shows how your taste matches theirs.
- Follow, share, or block from the buttons on their card.
- **Edit profile** got a refresh, with a live preview of your card as you change it.
- **People you might know** sits in a tidy section on your profile that you can open whenever you like.
- Profiles are organized into **Profile**, **Library**, and **Reviews**, and work nicely on phones.

## For You

- The new For You — Tonight's Programme, the time and mood dial, and **Go deeper** — is now live for everyone.
- Your streaming services button is back in the top corner, so picks lean toward what you can actually watch.
- On phones, the time and mood options fit neatly on one line.
