## New & Coming Soon

### The lobby
- New & Coming Soon has been rebuilt as a cinema lobby. A lit marquee at the top bills the week's lead opening, and it puts a film from your watchlist first when one is coming.
- **Arrivals** lists films in your country: what's out now, then this week, next week and later. Each film says whether it's in cinemas, in limited release, on a service you can stream it on, or to rent or buy.
- Point at a film (or tap it on your phone) to open it. You'll see the poster, the director and the synopsis, plus a ticket stub with **Go to page** and **Quick View**.
- Films from your watchlist come first and are marked with a bar on the left.

### The timetable
- **Timetable** shows a week of TV, with days across and channels down. Your shows come first, then streaming, then TV.
- New episodes from Netflix, Max, Disney+, Apple TV+, Prime Video and more are now included. Each one is listed under the service that carries it in your country, and shows you can't stream there are left out.
- A whole season dropping at once shows as one block. Premieres and finales are marked. Episode titles stay hidden until you tap **Show episode titles**.
- Channels from another country sit in their own **Air dates abroad** section, marked with that country's dates, because they may air later where you are.

### Follows your country
- The page opens straight away, with the lobby's outline showing while this week's listings load, and it's quicker on every visit after the first.
- Change your country from the sidebar and the page updates right away, with no reload.
- Release dates are always your country's own. If a film has no date where you are yet, it isn't listed.

## Watchlists

### Intermission
- When something on your lists has changed, a short **Intermission** card appears under your stats. It shows one title at a time: something you've already logged as seen, a show you were waiting on that has new episodes, or a saved title that's started or stopped streaming on your services.
- **Take a look** opens that list with **Tidy up** ready. Close the card and it stays hidden until something new comes up.
- It never nags about how long something has sat on a list.

### Tidy up
- Open **Tidy up** in any of your lists for suggestions, grouped as **Already seen**, **Your wait is over**, **Left your services**, **On your screens**, **Still on?** and **On the shelf a while**.
- Every suggestion is one tap: for example **Mark finished**, **Back to Watching**, **Finished it** or **Move to Up Next**. Nothing changes unless you choose it.
- **Mark all finished** clears every already-seen title at once, using the dates you logged them.
- Every change has **Undo**. A title you let go comes back in the same spot, with its note.
- **Keep** is remembered, so a title you've kept won't be suggested again.
- Only you see Tidy up and Intermission, even on a public list.

### More for this list
- Under your titles, **More for this list** suggests films and shows picked from what's already on the list. Each one sits on a ticket next to the title that brought it in.
- It stays closed until you open it, so your list loads as fast as ever.
- It never suggests anything already on your lists, or anything you've seen or passed.
- **Add** puts a pick on your list in one tap, with **Undo**. The panel tops itself up as you add, and **More picks** deals a fresh set.

### Adding and paging
- **Add a title** now sits above your titles: start typing, press Enter to add, or press **/** from anywhere on the page to jump to it. It replaces the **Add titles** button.
- **Delete list** moved into the **⋯** menu next to **Edit List**, away from Share.
- Lists show 18 titles a page, with page controls underneath. Your page stays put when you refresh.

## Favorites

- Favorites now show 18 a page too. Switching shelves, filters or your search takes you back to the first page.
- **Sort session** lists all your shelves, not just the first nine. Keys 1–9 still tick the first nine, the list scrolls for the rest, and a shelf you create mid-session scrolls into view. Ticking a shelf no longer makes the tiles jump.

## Signing up

### Start with what you love
- Signing up opens with **Three you love**: search for any film or show, or pick from a row that mixes classics with what's popular. You don't need an account for this part.
- Pick three to five titles. They're saved as likes that shape For You. They no longer get added to your Favorites, so your Favorites stay yours to curate.

### Three fields, not six
- Creating an account asks for your email, a username and a password. Your username is suggested from your email, and you can change it as you type.
- No first or last name, and no typing your password twice. Tap the eye to check it instead.

### The rest of your first night
- **The venue** shows your country in one step, with **Change** if you're watching from somewhere else.
- **Your screens** lets you tick the services you have, including Mubi, Criterion Channel and Kanopy where they're available. You can skip it.
- **Name on the stub** asks for your first and last name and the name to show on your profile, with an optional photo and a line about your taste. You can skip this too.
- Every answer prints onto your ticket as you go. Finished steps fold into a single line, and you can **Change** any of them.

### Tonight's feature
- The last step hands you one pick for tonight, based on what you loved and streaming in your country, with the reason it was picked.
- Already seen it? Tap **Seen it, next** for another. **Watch tonight** opens it on Watchly.
- If you leave partway through, signing in takes you back to the step you were on.

## Signing in

- The sign-in page has a new look. A cinema letterboard says **Still up?** late at night, or **Welcome back.** the rest of the day.
- Sign in with your email or username in the same box. There's no switch between the two anymore.
- **New here? Get your first ticket** takes you straight to signing up.

## Your profile

### Your last watch, as a ticket
- A ticket for the last title you logged now sits under your profile card, peeking out from the corner. Click it and the two swap places; click the card (or press Esc) to swap back.
- The ticket reads like a box-office receipt: the poster, the date you saw it, when it started, when you logged it, and the runtime. Tap the title or poster to open Quick View.
- Start times are worked out from when you logged it and the runtime, so they only show when they can be right: films with a known runtime, logged the same night.
- Visitors see the ticket only if your watched list is visible to them. They see the date and the runtime, never your clock times.
- Tap **Share** on your ticket to turn it into an Instagram story: the receipt with your @username as the customer, and a torn-off **Admit one** stub with a QR code that opens your profile.

### Taste, reviews and likes
- Your taste summary now learns a little from what you browse: titles you open from search or Tonight's Programme, and trailers you watch for 15 seconds or more. Each counts for a quarter of a save, and only nudges your genres and eras. Skipping a trailer never counts, and none of it is shown to visitors.
- **Review voice** on your profile shows your latest written review, instead of your best-rated one, and skips ratings with no words.
- **Likes** has a **Watchly Onboarding** filter for the titles you picked when you signed up. Filters you have no likes in are hidden. Signup picks no longer count as swipes in your stats.

## Emails

- Every email from Watchly has a new look that matches the app, and greets you by the name on your profile.
- Signing up sends one email: **Confirm your email**. It tells you the link works for 24 hours and that you have your first week to confirm. Your welcome arrives once you've confirmed.
- Confirming your email or restoring your account now takes one tap on the page the link opens. Work email link checkers can no longer use up your link before you get to it.
- If you ask to delete your account, the email shows the exact date it will be deleted, and what you'd keep if you restore it.

## Around the app

- **For You** opens straight away too, showing the reel's outline while your first trailers are picked.
- In **Settings**, **Who can see my watchlists** now says plainly what each choice means: a list you mark public can still be opened by anyone with its link.
- Page titles on Watchlists, Favorites, Swipe, Tonight's Programme, Discover and Settings now share the tall cinema type used on New & Coming Soon.
- A link that leads nowhere now lands on a **Not showing** page instead of a blank error. It has a search box, filled in from the words in the link when it has any, and quick ways back to Discover, your watchlists or home.
- Fixed title and cast pages that showed an error instead of loading.
- A title that briefly fails to load now offers **Try again**, and works on the next visit instead of staying broken for a day.
