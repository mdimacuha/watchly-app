## Profiles

- Tap **Followed by** on someone's profile to see everyone you follow who follows them.
- Names in **Followed by** now open that person's profile.

## Fixes

- Titles you review now appear on your **Watched** tab, dated to the day you reviewed them. Reviews you wrote before this update are there too.
- Titles you've reviewed no longer show up in your For You recommendations.
- If a title you just reviewed is still unfinished in one of your watchlists, tap **Mark as finished** right after you save your review.
- Deleting a review keeps the title in **Watched**. Remove it from there if you didn't watch it.
- **Taste DNA** on your profile now fills its card instead of leaving a gap at the bottom.
- Pages load faster across Watchly, your profile most of all.
- Switching back to **Favorites** or **Reviews** on a profile shows them right away instead of loading again.
