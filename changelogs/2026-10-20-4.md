## Watched

### Seen it, anywhere
- **Seen it** is on every title page, in Quick View, and on search results. Tap it, then say when you saw it, or tap **Skip** if it was today.
- Already seen? The button shows when ("Seen in 2011"), and tapping it lets you change the date or remove it from Watched.
- If the title is in one of your watchlists, **Seen it** marks it finished there too, and **Undo** puts everything back.

### Log past watches
- The **Admit one** ticket at the top of your Watched tab opens **Log a past watch**: search, pick a title, say roughly when, and move straight on to the next.
- The year you pick carries over to the next title, so logging a stretch of films from the same year is quick.

### When you saw it
- Tap the **Seen** date on any of your tickets to change it. Pick the year, then a month and day only if you remember them.
- Not sure at all? Choose **I don't remember** and the ticket waits in **Undated** until you add a date.
- Tickets now run in the order you saw them, so **No. 0001** is the first one on your timeline.
- Logged a few on the same day? Watched points it out and walks you through setting each date, one ticket at a time.
- Just marked something watched in For You? Tap **Change date** on the confirmation to set when you saw it.

### Tidying up
- Remove a ticket with the **×** in its corner. If it was finished in a watchlist, it goes back to Watching there.
- **Undo** puts back a ticket or an old date.
- **Show more** loads older tickets past your latest 60.
- **Download my data** now includes the dates you set.
