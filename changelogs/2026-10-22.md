## Sidebar

### Discover and My Profile
- The sidebar has two sections now. **Discover** holds every way to find something: For You, Tonight's Programme, Swipe and New & Coming Soon. **My Profile** holds your Watchlists and Favorites.
- A search box sits at the top. Type and press Enter to search from any page, or press `/` or Ctrl+K to jump to it.
- The page you're on lights up, along with the line leading to it in its section, so you can always see where you are.
- **New & Coming Soon** has a place in the sidebar ahead of its launch. The page itself is on its way.

### Tonight
- A ticket under the menu answers "what should I watch tonight?" with tonight's feature from Tonight's Programme, why it was picked and where it streams. Tap **Swap** for another pick.
- When a title you follow just arrived on one of your services, it takes the top of the ticket first.
- Tap the **i** beside **Tonight** to see where each part comes from, with a shortcut to change it in Settings.
- Below it, **On your ticket** shows the shows and films you're watching or waiting on, one tap from their pages. With nothing in progress, it shows the titles you opened last.

### Make it yours
- New **Sidebar** settings: show both halves of Tonight, just **Tonight's feature**, just **On your ticket**, or turn it **Off**. Your choices follow you to every device you sign in on.
- Try the experimental **Floating** sidebar: a slim rail off the edge of the screen that opens over the page when you point at it. Pin it to keep it open, and choose whether it's translucent, whether it moves fluidly, and how far it floats from the edge.
- The search box stays at the top of the sidebar while the rest scrolls, and the sidebar scrolls without a scrollbar in the way.

### Your region
- The bottom of the sidebar now says where you're browsing from ("You're currently in Philippines"), instead of a two-letter code. Click it to change region.

## Discover

- Search is now **Discover**, at its own address. Old search links and bookmarks still take you there, with your search intact.

## For You

- The reel no longer runs out. More trailers load well before you reach the end.
- Every visit starts a new mix: picks for your taste, titles that are **Popular today**, and now and then **Something different**.
- Titles you've already scrolled past rest for a week before they come back, so the reel keeps feeling new.

## Settings

- Watchly is dark only for now. Light mode is taking a break while we fix it, so the theme choice is gone from Settings.
- The **Profile banner from your favorites** switch is gone from Settings. Your profile keeps the banner as it is.
