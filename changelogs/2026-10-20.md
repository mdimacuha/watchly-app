## Watchlists

### A new look
- Each list now has a cover you can flip through: move across it, or scroll over it, to see what's inside.
- Cards show how much of a list you've watched, with a progress bar and how many hours you've watched out of the total.
- The page opens with your stats at a glance: what you're watching now, what's left to watch, what you've watched, and how many titles you've saved.
- Sort your lists by recently updated, newest, name, or size, and switch between grid and list views.
- Rows in list view open up to show more, and a wide view lays a list's posters out in a cascade.

### Inside a list
- **Hide unranked** shows only the titles you've ranked. It sits next to the view buttons and stays on when you share the link.
- Rewatching a title now counts as watched in your progress.
- Status columns on the board view have arrows to move between them.

## Favorites

### Shelves
- Group your favorites onto shelves, like "Comfort watches" or "Guilty pleasures". A favorite can sit on as many shelves as you like.
- Your shelves stand on a row of spines that grow as they fill. Tap one to open it, or drag a favorite onto it to shelve it.
- Give each shelf a spine colour, rename it, or delete it without losing the favorites on it.
- Not sure where to start? Watchly suggests shelves from what you've already loved.
- **Wide** view shows every shelf as its own row of posters.
- **Sort session** walks you through your favorites one at a time so you can shelve them quickly.

### Public or private
- Every shelf is private until you choose **Show on profile**.
- Public shelves appear on your profile even if you hide your favorites.

## Profiles

### Make it yours
- Choose up to six titles from your favorites and watchlists for **Feature presentation**, in the order you like. Your #1 gets the crown, and it becomes "Their #1" on your card too.
- Pick any accent colour: choose a preset, spin the colour wheel, or type a hex code. Watchly keeps very dark or very light picks readable.
- **Edit profile** now fits on screen, with your accent right under the live preview.

### Library
- **Library** now has **Watchlists**, **Favorites**, and **Watched** tabs.
- Watchlists use the same flip-through covers as your Watchlists page, and so does **Programmes**.
- Favorites show as shelves, with an **All favorites** row when your favorites are visible.
- What you've watched shows up as ticket stubs, grouped by month and numbered across your whole history.

### Reviews and privacy
- The **Reviews** tab now lists every review you've written, not just one.
- **Review voice** now shows which title your featured review is about.
- Private and followers-only profiles now show a clear locked page with a Follow button instead of "page not found".
- The Instagram story no longer includes your bio. It leads with your taste summary instead.

## Title pages

### A new layout
- Title pages are redesigned: a big poster, where to watch right under the title, and the trailer plays right on the page.
- A side panel keeps your lists, release details, and links within reach as you scroll.
- Videos, stills, cast, and crew have their own sections, and rows you can scroll sideways now have arrows.

### Notify me when
- Set an alert on any title to hear when it starts streaming in your country, leaves a service, or gets a new season.
- Alerts are checked daily and arrive in your notifications. Manage them all in Settings.

### Ratings and reviews
- Rate in half stars.
- Tick **Contains spoilers** when you review, and readers will need to tap **Show review** to read it.

## Where to watch

- When nothing streams in your country, Watchly shows the nearest country where it does, and says so.
- Collection and actor pages show what's available in your country, and titles that aren't say "Not in your region".
- Quick View is redesigned, and collections now open in Quick View too.

## Settings

- Settings is now one page, grouped by the questions you come with: **Who can see my stuff?**, **What will Watchly ping me about?**, **Is my account safe?**, and more.
- **What visitors see** previews your profile as a stranger or a follower, and warns you about anything you might not expect, like a public shelf.
- Every privacy option now says, in plain words, what it does.
- See the devices you're signed in on and sign any of them out, or sign out everywhere else at once.
- Review and unblock the people you've blocked.
- **Download my data** saves a copy of your lists, favorites, shelves, watched titles, reviews, follows, and alerts.

## Notifications

- You'll hear when people you follow review something, with their rating.
- Title alerts land in your notifications, and can be switched off in Settings.

## Fixes

- Adding a For You pick to a watchlist no longer clears the programme.
- Quick View now shows when a title is already in your watchlists or favorites.
- The search dropdown comes back when you return to the search box after opening a title.
- Reviews are no longer blocked because two ordinary words happened to run together.
- Long job titles in a title's crew list no longer overlap.
