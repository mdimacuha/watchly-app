## Notifications

### For you and Following
- Notifications now have two tabs: **For you** for new followers, follow requests and your title alerts, and **Following** for what people you follow are up to.
- The bell only counts things for you. Want friends' activity on it too? Turn on **Count it on the bell** in Settings.
- New notifications sit under **New**, and the rest are sorted into **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week** and **Earlier**.
- **Load older** takes you back further.

### One line per run
- When someone favorites or adds a run of titles, you get one line naming them, like "favorited The Incredibles, Ratatouille and 4 more", with their posters.
- Before, you saw only some of those titles, one notification at a time.
- Reviews show the stars they gave.
- A renamed list shows its new name.
- Collections and people they favorite now show by name instead of "a title".

### Privacy
- You're only told about lists, favorites and profiles you're allowed to see.
- Private lists and hidden favorites are never announced to followers.
- Notifications about things made private later disappear.

### Quicker to act on
- Accept or decline follow requests right from the notification.
- Tap someone's name or picture to open their profile, or tap a poster to open that title.
