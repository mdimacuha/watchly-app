# Global Rules

- Keep solutions simple and shippable.
- Do not over-engineer.
- Match the existing codebase.
- Keep routes/controllers thin.
- Separate business logic from transport.
- Validate all external input.
- Do not leak internal errors or secrets.
- Never edit applied migrations.
- Avoid `any`.
- No unnecessary abstractions.
